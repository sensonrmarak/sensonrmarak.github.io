📊 KEY FACTS
• Airbus A320neo maximum takeoff weight: 79 tonnes; liftoff at roughly 270 km/h
• Spoilers dump lift after touchdown so the aircraft's weight rests on the wheels and the brakes work (FAA Airplane Flying Handbook)
• Lift = C_L × ½ρV² × A, so doubling speed gives 4× the lift (NASA Glenn Research Center)
• Most of a modern turbofan's thrust comes from bypass air, not burning fuel; the CFM LEAP-1A's bypass ratio is about 11:1
• At 35,000 ft the air is about 31% as dense as at sea level and about −54 °C (ICAO Standard Atmosphere)
• IMD reported jet-stream winds of about 150 knots at 12.6 km over northwest India (4 January 2026)
• India's GAGAN system (ISRO + Airports Authority of India) sharpens satellite navigation for precise approaches
• About half of a Boeing 787's airframe, by weight, is carbon-fibre composite
