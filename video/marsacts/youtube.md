## Description

On 24 September 2014, a small Indian spacecraft fired its engine for 24 minutes in the shadow of Mars, alone and out of contact, and went into orbit. India became the first Asian nation to reach Mars orbit, and the first country to do it on its first attempt.

The whole mission cost about $74 million (around ₹450 crore). That's less than the budget of many Hollywood blockbusters.

So how did they do it? A smaller rocket and a clever slingshot around the Earth, a light spacecraft built from proven designs, and a team that built it in just 15 months. Along the way there was a valve fault, 300 days of silence, and one engine burn they couldn't afford to miss.
