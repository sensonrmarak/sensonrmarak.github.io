**Optional. If you add one, choose "India" (or your city).** Location is mainly used for place-based searches and travel or vlog content. For an educational video it has little or no effect on how YouTube recommends it. Recommendations depend mostly on the title, thumbnail, watch time and audience. Your real reach in India comes from the English (India) language setting, the UPSC keywords in the title and description, and Indian viewers watching. There is no downside to adding a general location like "India" or "New Delhi". Don't add your home address or a precise place if you want privacy.
